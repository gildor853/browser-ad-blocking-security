## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-28

- Analysis of advertising, tracking and third-party scripts as browser attack channels: malvertising, zero-click ad delivery, search-ad impersonation, scam ads, real-time bidding and script supply chain.
- Evidence chapter with 49 cited public sources (government guidance, incident research, measurements), including the evidence that weakens the case.
- Layered protection guide beyond ad blocking: updates, protective DNS, safe downloads, phishing-resistant sign-in, extension hygiene, isolation and mobile.
- Per-browser recommendations with verified extension IDs, Chrome, Edge and Firefox policy examples, and protective DNS endpoints.
- Counterarguments, methodology and a short Czech summary.
